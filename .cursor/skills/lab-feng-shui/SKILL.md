# Lab feng shui

The Overview is the reference. Important numbers jump out. A newcomer should get the point in a few seconds without a wall of text.

## One insight per screen

- Giant number or three-word headline first.
- One short subtext line, not a paragraph.
- Put methodology, caveats, and “how to read this” behind a disclosure.
- Do not add confidence badges, evidence dumps, or research chrome on the face of a presentation page.

## Fill the card

- A chart with two bars (Sex, Hispanic origin) must occupy the square the way four- and six-item charts do. Grow the tracks. Do not leave a half-empty panel.
- Logos and portraits go to the box: logos `contain` on a brand field; photos `cover` with a sensible focal point.
- Default Spaces tiles to logos (YouTube, Fortnite, Pokémon, Roblox, Minecraft), not gameplay or UI screenshots. Extra investigation happens after click.

## Collapse the chrome

- Filters start collapsed. Type chips plus search can stay visible; platform / age / topic live under “More filters”.
- Compare is two dropdowns, two columns. Left generation, right generation, one topic. Never a multi-select chip pile.
- Gender findings are cards: metric + title visible; interpretation on hover or focus.

## Copy that can be presented

- Credit the source in a kicker (example: Snapchat × Omnicom), then get out of the way.
- Keep the line that earns the laugh (“Timing is the whole joke”). Cut the rest.
- If a sentence could be skipped in a live read, it does not belong on the first surface.

## When editing

1. Ask what should jump out at 100% zoom.
2. Cut until the remaining type can be read aloud in under ten seconds.
3. Check dark theme hover contrast (no black-on-black labels).
4. Keep the graph (counts, IDs, evidence records). Change the chrome, not the source of truth.
